Getting started

Studio

A drag-and-drop canvas for building a data pipeline visually. Drag blocks from the left palette onto the canvas, wire them together by connecting their ports, then press Run pipeline to queue every step in order.

The Assistant

A chat panel (bottom-right of the Dashboard) you can ask questions or give plain-language instructions to — it can inspect your files and queue pipeline steps for you. Add a Gemini API key in Settings to turn it on.

Queueing tasks

The "Queue a task" form on the Dashboard runs one pipeline step at a time (import, annotate, filter, chart, etc.) against your CSV/HTML files without needing the visual canvas. Watch its progress in the Dashboard's "Recent tasks" or the Studio's "Active tasks".
